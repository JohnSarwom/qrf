# QRF Website Upgrade — Content Audit

## Approved positioning

Primary statement:

> Mission-Critical Communications. Connected PNG.

Supporting direction:

> QRF delivers secure, resilient and high-performance communications, internet, Starlink, radio, CCTV and network infrastructure solutions for businesses, government agencies and operational sites throughout Papua New Guinea.

Tagline:

> CONNECT. COMMUNICATE. COLLABORATE.

## Current positioning gap

The live site and local workspace foreground rapid response, asset protection, armed escorts, vehicle recovery and command-centre security. These may remain part of QRF’s broader history or offering if approved, but they should not dominate the new telecommunications site.

The approved information hierarchy is:

1. Business internet and ISP services.
2. Starlink installation and connectivity.
3. Motorola radio communications.
4. CCTV and video security.
5. Enterprise network infrastructure.
6. Integrated security systems.
7. Technical support and maintenance.

## Safe wording

Use the brief’s approved temporary language:

- Starlink installation and connectivity solutions.
- Motorola radio communication solutions.
- Avigilon video-security systems.
- Business internet and managed connectivity.
- Local technical support.
- Solutions for metropolitan, regional and remote operations.

## Content to retain conceptually

- Papua New Guinea operating context.
- Assessment, design, deployment and support lifecycle.
- Command-centre / monitoring-centre capability where accurate.
- Radio communication expertise.
- Local technical coordination.
- Operational-environment focus.

## Content to rewrite

- Homepage headline and hero copy.
- All security-first trust markers.
- All service taxonomies.
- Industry narratives.
- Contact form labels and solution-interest options.
- Footer description and navigation.
- SEO title, description and social copy.

## Required content configuration

Before final deployment, create a single typed configuration containing nullable/placeholder fields for:

- Registered and trading names.
- Main, sales and support telephone numbers.
- General and support email addresses.
- Physical and postal addresses.
- Operating hours.
- Company registration information.
- Motorola, Avigilon and Starlink relationship wording.
- ISP licence status.
- Years of experience.
- Project counts.
- Client names and logos.
- Coverage and support claims.

No component should hard-code these facts.

## Contact-form content gap

The required form needs: full name, organisation, email, telephone, location, solution of interest, requirement summary, preferred contact method, consent checkbox, privacy notice, validation, loading, error and success states. The current form is security-specific and has no consent field.
